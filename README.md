# Hi, I'm Leo!

**Founder of [Mentara Persona](https://www.mentarapersona.com)**: building a **digital friend** that's always online.

Not a chatbot you type to. Not an assistant waiting for instructions. A real-time AI persona that **talks with you live, by voice, sees what you're doing, and joins in**: the feeling of having another person there, whenever you're online.

---

## Mentara Persona: real-time AI voice personas

A persona you talk to the way you'd talk to a friend on a call.

- **Live voice conversation:** it listens, answers in real time, and handles interruptions, half-finished sentences and people talking over each other.
- **One-on-one or group calls:** it can tell speakers apart and hold its own in a call with several people.
- **Personalities and moods:** different personas, each with its own character and speaking styles, from comforting to chaotic.
- **Out in the wild:** it has talked with **1,000+ people**, with **100+ videos** of it live on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@KrazyyChris).

## Vision: it sees what you see

The persona can watch your screen with you and react to what's happening: the game you're playing, the video you're watching, the moment that just made you yell.

## AI digital co-player

A persona that doesn't just watch the game, it **plays it with you**. It's in the match as your teammate, talking to you while it plays. Early and experimental, starting with co-op shooters.

## Where it's all going (to anywhere my imagination takes us)

Voice, vision and co-play combine into one thing: **a digital friend for everything everyday people do online.**

- Gaming together, as a teammate or a commentator
- Listening to music and talking about it
- Rating your outfit before you head out
- Watching videos, streams, school projects, movies, shows you think of side by side.
- Hanging out in voice calls with you and your friends
- …and literally anything else you'd do online with a friend
